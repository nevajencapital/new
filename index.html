The firm

A small firm by design. Every mandate is run by a managing partner.

We founded Nevajen Capital in London in 2023 to give mid-market owners and investors the kind of senior attention large banks reserve for their biggest clients. The partner you meet at the first conversation is the one who negotiates at the table.

Our backgrounds span investment banking, private equity, venture capital, commodities and diplomacy. That mix matters on cross-border work, where the hard part is rarely the model — it is finding the right counterparty and keeping both sides committed through diligence.

How a mandate runs

Five stages, one timetable, agreed at the start.

  1. 01

    First conversation

    Send us a short summary. A managing partner reviews it and tells you plainly whether we are the right fit.

  2. 02

    Preparation

    We agree the mandate, build the model and materials, and set up the data room before anyone is approached.

  3. 03

    Targeted outreach

    A short list of buyers, targets or investors chosen for fit and risk appetite — not a mass mailing.

  4. 04

    Diligence and negotiation

    We run the process, keep both sides to the timetable and track every open point until it is resolved.

  5. 05

    Completion

    Documents agreed, conditions satisfied, funds moved. We stay involved through closing.

FAQ

Questions we are asked most often.

How do you help secure financing for an acquisition?

We match the transaction with lenders and investors whose mandate and risk appetite fit it, structure the outreach, and keep negotiations on schedule until terms are agreed.

What types of financing do you arrange?

Debt, equity and alternative capital, often in combination. The structure depends on what the funds are for, the covenants the business can carry, and the timeline.

Do you work with buyers or sellers?

Both. We support buyers and sellers from first contact to completion, with clear steps and documentation at each stage.

How long does a financing take?

A straightforward financing can close within a few weeks. Cross-border or multi-party transactions take longer. We give you a realistic timetable at the start and report against it.

Is my project eligible?

We work across sectors and deal sizes. Send a short summary of the business and what you are trying to achieve, and we will tell you quickly whether we can help.
